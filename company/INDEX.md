# Acme company knowledge base (index)

Each line: document file - what it covers. The operator reads only the documents relevant to a task.

- systems.md - Which internal system to use for what, URLs, and how to sign in.
- hiring-policy.md - The hiring bar (must-have criteria) for each open role and how to record screening decisions.
- interview-procedure.md - How to schedule, reschedule and confirm interviews; panel rules; invite email template.
- communication-policy.md - Rules for emailing candidates and colleagues, what needs human approval, what the AI operator must never do (offers, compensation).
- org-directory.md - Who is who: HR Head, hiring managers, interviewers and their emails.
