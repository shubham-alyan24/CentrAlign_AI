# Interview procedure

## First-round interviews (Round 1 - Technical)
- Every Shortlisted candidate gets one **Round 1 - Technical** interview (45 minutes).
- The interviewer must be on the job's interview panel (see the Jobs page in HireDesk or org-directory.md).
- Book the **earliest free slot** across the panel in the next 5 working days. Check calendars first; one candidate per slot.
- Booking an interview in HireDesk automatically moves the candidate to "Interview Scheduled".

## Rescheduling
- Use the Reschedule form on the interview's page in HireDesk (same interviewer) and give the reason.
- Pick the earliest free slot that satisfies the candidate's stated constraints. If none fits, ask a human.

## Confirming with the candidate
After booking or rescheduling, email the candidate from Acme Mail and **Cc the interviewer**. Use this template:

    Subject: Interview confirmation - <Job title> - Acme
    Hi <First name>,

    Your <Round> interview for the <Job title> role is scheduled on <Day, DD Mon YYYY> at <HH:MM> IST (45 minutes)
    with <Interviewer name>. A video link will be shared a day before.

    Please reply if you need to reschedule.

    Regards,
    Acme Talent Team

For reschedules, start the body with one line acknowledging their request.
