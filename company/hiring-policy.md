# Hiring policy

## JOB-101 Backend Engineer (Platform team)
A candidate is **Shortlisted** only if ALL must-haves are met:
1. At least **2 years** of professional software experience.
2. **Python** plus at least one of **Django** or **FastAPI**.
3. Notice period of **60 days or less**.

Nice to have (does not affect shortlisting): AWS, Docker, PostgreSQL.

## JOB-102 Data Analyst (Analytics team)
Must-haves: 1+ year experience, SQL, one BI tool (Tableau / Power BI / Looker).

## JOB-103 Sales Associate (Sales team)
Must-haves: 1+ year B2B sales experience, CRM experience.

## Recording screening decisions in HireDesk
- Every new application must be in HireDesk with: full name, email, phone (+91 format), job, years of experience, notice period (days), key skills, source = "Email application" (keep "Referral" for referrals) and the resume PDF.
- Meets all must-haves -> set stage **Shortlisted** (note: which criteria were met).
- Misses any must-have -> set stage **Rejected** with a note naming the criterion that was not met.
- Do NOT email rejected candidates. HR sends rejection emails in a weekly batch.
- Only process applications for the role named in the request; leave other roles' applications untouched.
